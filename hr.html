Paid Module: Building a Performance-Based Organization

An employee is an investment and employment is an exchange in value. You cannot hire an employee without a clear, itemized list of what the employee will bring to the table in exchange for his salary

If it's a sales position, there has to be a sales quota juxtaposed against his salary. If it's an admin position, a checklist of tasks and responsibilities have to be clearly outlined with a clear metric on the desired level of performance. If it's a position in operation, there should be a clear quantitative metric of the required output.
