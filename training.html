What Can be Taught vs. What Cannot be Taught

Each skill has a different time frame to acquire. Language Skills, the capacity for analytical thought, problem-solving and analytical skills are built through years and even decades of "brain train", it cannot be learned in a 3 day or 3 week seminar, no matter how good your trainor is. But there are skills that can be acquired through training provided that your employee has a certain set of capabilities on hand that he can build on. We will teach you how to identify which skills can be taught upon employment and which ones your employee should already have upon hiring.

The Pre-requisites of a Skill Set

Each skill set has a pre-requisite. If an employee seems promising but he doesn't have the skill set you immediately need, don't use your gut to decide. Just identify if the applicant has the pre-requisites of the skill you need. If he has it, he will learn the skill in no time. But first, you have to know what those pre-requisites are. This module teaches you what those are.
