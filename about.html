FAQs

How does the Maid-on-Demand cleaning service work?
MOD cleaning services provide fractional employment of Cleaners to households only at a time and date the homeowner needs the cleaning service. It allows clients to have clean homes without the need to employ a full-time, stay-in maid. This is more cost-efficient in the long run, apart from being safer because the client-cleaner relationship is maintained at a professional distance and the cleaning lady has no free access to the home when the homeowner is not at home.
How does The Blue Pages website work?
Clients who need blue collar work or odd chores done around the house may request for the contact details of the selected worker available for viewing in The Blue Pages website.
How does the Power Tutorial website work?
