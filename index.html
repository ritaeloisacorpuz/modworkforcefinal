What is MODworkforce?

MOD Workforce is a website that provides references and resources to small business owners to make their start-up business work.
Because the needs of small business owners are very different from conglomerates, what usually works for big corporations is a wrong move for a small business. That is why this website endorses "Modularization" as the small businessman’s formula for success. Modularization simply means proper scaling and this is the correct approach to a small business when you are not wealthy nor well-connected. "Modularizing" the availment of services, supplies and manpower is the key to allowing an entrepreneur to scale their business in pace with their growth. It is the only way to avoid bankruptcy before a small businessman achieves the positive cashflow necessary to make that jump into the big leagues.
MOD Workforce helps small businesses thrive in the MODern world through MODularization of service availment and making use of Manpower On Demand services. Because the future of MODern work is MODular.
