What is an independent contractor? An independent contractor is a self-employed individual hired to perform specific work or services under a written contract or agreement only for a certain duration. He is not a traditional employee because independent contractors maintain control over how, when, and where the work is performed, rather than having the hiring entity direct their day-to-day methods. This way, independent contractors can work for multiple clients at a time and are not bound to just one employer or client, and thus earn more. At the same time, the company is not bound to provide them with the usual employee benefits like paying for a portion of their SSS benefits, giving them retirement pay or paid time off. Independent contractors are responsible for paying their own self-employment taxes and do not receive employee benefits.
